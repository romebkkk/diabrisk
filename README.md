## 🌟 Why this matters
Type 2 Diabetes mellitus is a global chronic pandemic affecting over 500 million adults. In Spain alone, more than 5 million people live with diabetes, and **nearly half are undiagnosed**. 

Prediabetes is **completely asymptomatic yet 100% reversible**: landmark randomized clinical trials (DPP & DPS) proved that lifestyle modifications (5-7% weight reduction + 150 min/week of moderate physical activity) reduce the progression to diabetes by **58%**, surpassing metformin.

**DiabRisk** provides population-level early risk stratification without blood draws, empowering citizens to know their real 10-year probability and take action early.

---

## ⚡ Key Modules

1. **📊 FINDRISC Risk Engine (WHO & Spanish PREDAPS Cohort):**
   - 8 evidence-based clinical parameters: Age, BMI, sex-specific waist circumference (<94/102 cm men; <80/88 cm women), physical activity, daily fruits/vegetables, antihypertensive treatment, history of high glucose/gestational diabetes, and family history.
   - Stratified 10-year risk output: Low (1%), Slightly elevated (4%), Moderate (17%), High (33%), and Very High (50%).

2. **⚡ Lifestyle Reversal Simulator (DPP Trial):**
   - Interactive sliders showing immediate FINDRISC score drops and relative risk reductions upon losing 5-10 kg or incorporating 30 min of daily walking.

3. **🩸 ADA 2024 Glycemic Interpreter & Local Diary:**
   - Interprets Fasting Plasma Glucose (FPG), 2-hour Postprandial Glucose, and HbA1c against clinical thresholds.
   - In-browser persistent diary saved locally with zero cloud telemetry.

## 🔬 Clinical Evidence & Standards
- **Finnish Diabetes Risk Score (FINDRISC):** Lindström J, Tuomilehto J. Diabetes Care. 2003.
- **Spanish Validation (PREDAPS / DE-PLAN):** Soriguer F, et al. Diabetologia.
- **Diabetes Prevention Program (DPP):** Knowler WC, et al. N Engl J Med. 2002;346(6):393-403 (58% risk reduction).
- **Standards of Care in Diabetes — 2024:** American Diabetes Association (ADA).

## 🇪🇸 Español

### ¿Por qué importa?
La diabetes tipo 2 destruye silenciosamente los vasos sanguíneos, los riñones y la vista. Millones de personas padecen prediabetes sin saberlo porque no produce dolor. Sin embargo, la prediabetes **se puede revertir**: perder un 5-7 % del peso y caminar 30 minutos al día frena la diabetes en casi 6 de cada 10 personas.

**DiabRisk** te dice tu riesgo real en 2 minutos sin pinchazos y te enseña cómo bajarlo antes de que sea tarde.
